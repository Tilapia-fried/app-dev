# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

**Stranger Things** is a popular *science fiction horror drama* set in the 1980s. It follows a group of young friends in Hawkins, Indiana, as they uncover supernatural mysteries and secret government experiments.

---

## Main Characters
* **Eleven (Jane Hopper)** - A young girl with psychokinetic abilities.
* **Mike Wheeler** - The loyal leader of the friend group.
* **Jim Hopper** - The chief of police in Hawkins.

## Top 3 Favorite Seasons
1. Season 1 (The Vanishing of Will Byers)
2. Season 4 (Vecna's Curse)
3. Season 2 (The Mind Flayer)
